# Stakeholder Analysis

## Key High-Influence & High-Interest Stakeholders

1. **S01 - Head of the IT Department (Sponsor):** Holds final budget authority and determines whether the project meets departmental standards. Receives primary PM attention through bi-weekly milestone reviews and demo checkpoints.
2. **S02 - Supervising Lecturer (Project Manager):** Directly manages daily team activities, tracks risks, and oversees technical quality. Demands active attention to ensure schedule alignment across the 14-week lifecycle.

## Veto Stakeholder

**S08 - Data Protection Officer:** Holds high influence despite low day-to-day interest. If student data retention, security, or privacy handling does not meet institutional compliance guidelines prior to week 14, the Data Protection Officer has the authority to veto and block the live 50-student pilot.
